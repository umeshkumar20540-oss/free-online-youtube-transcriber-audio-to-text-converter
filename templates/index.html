Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this online YouTube transcriber free to use?

Yes, WhisperFlow is 100% free with no subscription, no sign-up, and no credit card required. Because it runs on your device's GPU and CPU, there are no server costs or artificial audio length caps.

Can I upload MP3, WAV, and MP4 files directly?

Absolutely. You can drag and drop any audio or video file including MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, MP4, WebM, and FLAC. The audio track is decoded in real time using the browser's Web Audio API.

How does client GPU and CPU transcription work?

Using Hugging Face Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime Web, the OpenAI Whisper neural network is compiled directly into WebGPU compute shaders that execute on your graphics card. If WebGPU is not supported by your browser, it automatically runs across your CPU cores via multi-threaded WebAssembly (WASM).

How do I export subtitles for YouTube, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve?

Once transcription completes, click the "Export As..." button and select Subtitles (.srt) or WebVTT (.vtt). You can also export timestamped plain text, JSON with segment bounds, CSV spreadsheets, and structured Markdown notes.
